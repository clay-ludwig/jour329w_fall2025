Beat Book Guide
December 15, 2025

---

For this assignment, I got the LLM to do all of my work for me! >:)

I'm half-joking, but I did let it take the reigns more than I'm usually comfortable with.

I gave Opus 4.5 in Copilot the `combined.txt` file as context, and then asked it to create a high-level narrative guide based on my notes from the semester. I specifically asked it to "weigh" the more recent assignments more, such as the different and final beat books, since those were the most polished and contained some of the more important "breakthroughs" I've had this semester.

The draft that Opus made can be accessed in the `0_beat_book_process_guide.md` file.

Out of curiosity, I asked if it could rewrite the guide more in my voice (given the notes it had access to), and Claude thought *very* long and hard about how to do this.

At one point, this was of the things Claude internally thought to itself about my notes:
```
Often mentions time ("it's 3 a.m.") and emotional states
```
I feel a little attacked, but I'll sleep well knowing I've given Claude an easy task compared to Cat's notes.

Anyway, that did not work well at all. It completely overemphasized certain parts of my writing, to the point where I didn't even feel like I was reading an actual guide (it read more like a Reddit post or something LOL). I guess I'll also sleep well knowing I can't be replaced by an LLM (yet).

I undid those changes and opted instead to manually refine and reword certain parts of the original guide it created. That can be found in the `1_beat_book_process_guide.md` file. In general, Claude actually did a good job here, but I did simplify some of the language and remove/generalize some overly specific examples.

The Copilot conversation summarization can be found in the `guide_copilot.md` file (since a `copilot.md` file already existed).
