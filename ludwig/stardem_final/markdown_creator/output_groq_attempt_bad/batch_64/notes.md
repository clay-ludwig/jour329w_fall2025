## Story 1 – “Caroline County Board of Ed declares last day of school, discuss virtual instruction” (April 8 2025)

**People / Titles / Roles**
- **Derek Simmons** – Superintendent, Caroline County Public Schools  
- **Robert Wielgosz** – Supervisor of Technology, Caroline County Public Schools  
- **Erica McDonald** – Supervisor of Special Education, Caroline County Public Schools  
- **Dr. Robert Willoughby** – Director of Administrative Services, Caroline County Public Schools  
- **Ahmad Garnett** – Author, Star Democrat (agarnett@chespub.com)

**Schools / Institutions / Organizations**
- Caroline County Board of Education  
- Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS)  
- Kajeet (hotspot provider)  
- Google (Google Meet platform)  

**Key Dates**
- **April 1, 2025** – Board of Education meeting where virtual‑instruction options were discussed.  
- **June 17, 2025** – Declared last day of the extended school year (half‑day for students, full day for teachers).  
- **2025‑2026 school year** – Planned three allotted inclement‑weather days.

**Enrollment / Demographic Numbers**
- **576** multilingual learners (ELs)  
- **569** students with Individualized Education Programs (IEPs)  
- **116** homeless students  

**Budget / Cost Figures**
- **700** Kajeet hotspot devices needed over five years → **$736,000** for students, **$35,000** for teachers.  
- Restoring Google Meet breakout‑room feature → **$30,000–$50,000** per year.  

**Programs / Initiatives / Policies**
- Exploration of virtual instruction for inclement‑weather days (ultimately deemed not feasible).  
- Kajeet hotspot program (used during pandemic).  
- Requirement (state law) that all staff and students have necessary devices and Wi‑Fi for virtual days.  

**Quotes**
- *“The structure we tried to take with the presentation was a condensed version of some the requirements that the school system would have to meet if the county were to entertain virtual weather days.”* – **Derek Simmons**  
- *“When given the opportunity to use Kajeet, some students would use the hotspot for personal uses like video games and movies… This overloaded the hotspots and caused delays.”* – **Robert Wielgosz**  
- *“It’s very hard to keep students engaged, especially for four hours of synchronous instruction.”* – **Erica McDonald**  
- *“Our teachers work really hard… the law does say that we may negotiate with our teachers’ union.”* – **Dr. Robert Willoughby**  
- *“I don’t see how as a county, with the resources we have right now, that this puts us in a great position to be able to hit all the marks to be able to pull this off effectively.”* – **Derek Simmons**
