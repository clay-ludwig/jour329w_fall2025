**Article:** *Chesapeake Culinary Center offers education everyone* – 19 Feb 2025 (Star Democrat, Easton, MD)

| Category | Details |

| **Institution / Organization** | **Chesapeake Culinary Center** – nonprofit organization that provides cooking, budgeting, and nutrition education. Located in **Denton, Caroline County, MD**. |
| **Key Personnel** | • **David Murray** – Operations Manager *and* Head Chef of the Chesapeake Culinary Center.<br>• **Angel Perez** – Programming and Events Manager. |
| **Background (personnel)** | - Murray’s first restaurant job was **at Emerson House Restaurant in Denton (Caroline County)** while in high school.<br>- He later attended culinary school before returning to run the center. |
| **Programs / Initiatives** | - **Cooking & Budgeting Classes** for all ages (free options available).<br>- **Healthy‑eating & shopping** workshops (focus on budgeting, bulk buying, meal prep, food preservation).<br>- **Mini series** (less‑intensive sessions) for all ages, e.g., kids’ pasta and cupcake classes.<br>- Planned **expansion** of class offerings to include **bartending and serving courses**.<br>- Classes offered in **English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole**. |
| **Target Audiences** | • Senior centers on the Eastern Shore.<br>• Young parents and new parents.<br>• General community members seeking budget‑friendly cooking skills.<br>• Children (via mini‑series). |
| **Geographic Reach** | - Currently serves **Caroline County** (headquartered in Denton).<br>- Intent to **expand outside Caroline County** to other Eastern Shore areas (per Angel Perez). |
| **Quotes** | • *David Murray*: “I’ve had the opportunity to do some classes with senior centers on the Eastern Shore, young parents, new parents… we always get feedback on how helpful it was for them to be able to learn how to shop on a budget and just healthy cooking tips and tricks.”<br>• *Murray*: “My first restaurant job at high school really kind of sparked things for me… I started by washing dishes and then worked my way up. Then I went to culinary school.”<br>• *Murray*: “People are very surprised at how much money they can save when they are able to buy something in bulk.”<br>• *Angel Perez*: “Our main focus is to help people to understand the importance of healthy eating habits, but also how to cook, how to manage your budget, how to also utilize the time you spend in the kitchen to spend time with the family at the same time, and make that moment family time.” |
