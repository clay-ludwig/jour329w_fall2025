**Story 1 – “Denton Elementary principal earns doctorate” (Jan. 17 2024)**  
- **People**  
  - **Dr. Sarah Crebs‑Oberdorf** – Principal, Denton Elementary School (appointed June 2022). Earned a doctorate in Contemporary Literacy Studies from Salisbury University.  
  - **Dr. Derek Simmons** – Superintendent, Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS). Attended Crebs‑Oberdorf’s dissertation defense and provided several quotes.  
- **Institutions / Organizations**  
  - **Denton Elementary School** (Caroline County).  
  - **Salisbury University** – Granted the doctorate.  
  - **Caroline County Board of Education** – Recognized the achievement at its Jan. 9 meeting.  
- **Dissertation**  
  - Title: *“An Inquiry Into a Collaborative Teacher Study Group: How do Elementary Teachers of Reading talk about the Intersection Between Race and Reading Instruction and Assessment?”*  
- **Quotes (Superintendent Derek Simmons)**  
  - “Sarah did a fantastic job with her defense… Her dissertation was very well done.”  
  - “Once … you get done answering all the questions, the best part is … they come to tell you, ‘Congratulations, Doc.’”  
  - “It’s a lot of years of hard work and it pays off.”  
- **Career background of Dr. Crebs‑Oberdorf**  
  - Former special‑education teacher in Caroline County and other districts.  
  - Former assistant principal, Ridgely Elementary School.

**Story 4 – “State test scores: Mid‑Shore school districts see mixed results” (Aug. 26 2025)**  
- **People**  
  - **Derek Simmons** – Superintendent, Caroline County Public Schools; provided a statement on test‑score results.  
- **Test‑score data for Caroline County (2024‑2025 MCAP)**  
  - **English**  
    - Proficiency rate: **49 %** (up **3.5 %** year‑to‑year).  
    - State rank: **14th** out of 24 districts.  
    - Tied for the **fifth‑highest** year‑to‑year improvement among Mid‑Shore counties.  
  - **Mathematics**  
    - Proficiency rate: **22.2 %** (up **2.5 %** year‑to‑year).  
    - State rank: **15th**.  
    - Caroline’s growth rate **outpaced the state average**.  
  - **Science (5th grade)**  
    - Proficiency rate: **19 %**.  
    - State rank: **18th**.  
  - **Science (8th grade)**  
    - Proficiency rate: **23.8 %** (up **4.5 %** year‑to‑year).  
    - State rank: **16th**.  
- **Quotes (Superintendent Derek Simmons)**  
  - “I’m very encouraged to see the district’s growth rate outpace the state’s.”  
  - “Our sustained focus on what matters, and consistency around evidence‑based approaches will continue our growth.”
