## Caroline County Education – Data Breach (PowerSchool)  
**Story:** “Local school districts affected by PowerSchool data breach” – 22 Jan 2025  

### People & Titles
- **Derek Simmons** – Superintendent, Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS)  

### Schools & Organizations
- **Caroline County Public Schools** – district affected by the PowerSchool breach.  
- **PowerSchool** – vendor providing the student information system (SIS) for CCPS (and Dorchester & Talbot counties).  

### Dates & Timeline
| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| PowerSchool became aware of the breach | **28 Dec 2024** |
| PowerSchool notified Caroline County district | **7 Jan 2025** |
| CCPS (and other districts) notified families | Early **January 2025** (specific date not given) |
| Article publication | **22 Jan 2025** |

### Issue Details
- **Nature of breach:** Hackers obtained personal information including **names, dates of birth, medical information, and Social Security numbers** of some students, educators, and families.  
- **Scope:** Affected districts in **Caroline, Dorchester, and Talbot counties**.  

### Official Statements & Quotes
- **Derek Simmons (CCPS Superintendent):**  
  > “PowerSchool has also made a commitment to conduct a thorough notification process to all impacted individuals. We intend to hold PowerSchool to these commitments.”  

### Responses & Actions
- **PowerSchool’s response:**
  - Immediate activation of cybersecurity response protocols and engagement of a cross‑functional response team (including senior leadership and third‑party cybersecurity experts).  
  - Offering **two years of complimentary identity protection services** for all impacted students and educators.  
  - Offering **two years of complimentary credit monitoring services** for all adult students and educators whose information was involved, regardless of whether an SSN was exfiltrated.  
  - Working with a credit‑reporting agency to provide the credit monitoring.  
  - Stated that the incident is contained and security protocols have been enhanced.  

### Programs / Initiatives Mentioned
- **Identity protection and credit monitoring services** (provided by PowerSchool to affected individuals).
