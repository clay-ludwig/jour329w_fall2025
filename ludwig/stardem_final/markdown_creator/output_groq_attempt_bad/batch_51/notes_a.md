## Caroline County Education – Batch 51 Notes  

### Story 2 – “Phone pouches improving student focus, discipline in Caroline, district says” (Jan 15 2025)

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **People** | **Derek Simmons** – Superintendent, Caroline County Public Schools. |
| **Organizations / Schools** | **Caroline County Public Schools** – the district that piloted the phone‑pouch program in its middle schools. |
| **Program / Initiative** | **Phone‑pouch pilot** (cell‑phone lock‑away system) – implemented at **both Caroline County middle schools** (schools not named). |
| **Vendor / Supplier** | **Yondr** – company that manufactures the phone pouches. |
| **Program Mechanics** | • Each student receives an individual pouch that travels with them from home to school.<br>• Phones are locked in the pouch at the beginning of the school day and unlocked at the end with a magnetic device.<br>• If a student forgets the pouch, the phone is placed in the school office for the day.<br>• Every classroom is equipped with a wired phone for emergencies.<br>• Teachers are given a special pair of scissors to cut through the pouches if necessary.<br>• Students with health needs may access phones for medical purposes under “special services.” |
| **Potential Expansion** | Discussions about extending the pouch program to **ninth‑grade students** in future years (no decision made). |
| **Evaluation Timeline** | District plans to have a solid understanding of the program’s successes and shortcomings **by the end of the school year** (2025). |
| **Reported Successes** | • **21 % reduction** in discipline incidents (as reported by Superintendent Simmons).<br>• Teachers note higher classroom attentiveness and reduced need to police phone use.<br>• Reported increase in **pass rates** (no specific numbers provided). |
| **Teacher/Staff Quote** | “We've been pleased with the outcome… You see attentiveness much higher in classrooms, kids learning how to talk to each other, even in the cafeteria. It's been really cool to see at the middle schools.” – *Derek Simmons* |
| **Teacher/Staff Quote on Discipline** | “We've seen about a 21 % reduction in discipline incidents.” – *Derek Simmons* |
| **Teacher/Staff Quote on Instruction** | “With the pouches they don't have to be the cellphone police.” – *Derek Simmons* |
| **Reported Difficulties / Challenges** | • Some students have attempted to tamper with or break the pouches.<br>• Pouches are **not indestructible**; tampering is detectable.<br>• Some parents object because they prefer direct communication with their child.<br>• No student survey results are available yet; district plans to consider student attitudes in future decisions. |
| **Additional Quote (Challenges)** | “They're not indestructible, they're not the end all be all, but they are a great preventative tool to put in place.” – *Derek Simmons* |
| **Quotes on Overall Impact** | “I think overall, we're seeing a positive impact on the academics, on the social skills piece. If we asked kids if they'd rather have access to their devices, I'm sure they would.” – *Derek Simmons* |
