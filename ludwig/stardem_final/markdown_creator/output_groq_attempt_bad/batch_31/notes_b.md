### Policies, Programs & Initiatives Discussed  

1. **FY26 Comprehensive Maintenance Plan**  
   - Objective: Ensure effective learning environments, minimize disruptions, preserve property value, maximize facility efficiency, and provide risk‑management/preventive‑maintenance foundation.  
   - Required by the **Interagency Committee on School Construction**.  

2. **Operation School Nutrition Programs Policy 9910**  
   - Updates a 1980s‑era policy; renames “school lunch program” to **“nutrition program”** to include breakfast.  
   - Clarifies pricing for meals under the **Community Eligibility Provision** and for a‑la‑carte items.  

3. **Policy 55080 – Service Animals**  
   - Aligns district practices with **ADA** regulations.  
   - Defines “service animal” (typically dogs; miniature horses only in specific mobility‑related cases).  
   - Explicitly **excludes** emotional‑support animals and therapy animals from the definition.  

4. **Citizens’ Advisory Committee (2025‑2027)**  
   - Composed of parents and community members.  
   - Provides feedback to the Board of Education.  
   - Nominees presented by **Superintendent Derek Simmons** and approved by the board.

### Issues & Themes Highlighted  

- **Rising construction/maintenance costs** (inflation, labor, potential tariff impacts).  
- **Policy modernization** for nutrition services and ADA compliance (service‑animal definitions).  
- **Enrollment trends**: slight overall decline, elementary gains, middle/high school losses, notable growth in home‑school enrollment.  
- **Community engagement** via the Citizens’ Advisory Committee.
