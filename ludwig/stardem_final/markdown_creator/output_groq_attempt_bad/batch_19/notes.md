## Caroline County – Education‑Related Details Extracted from Batch 19  

### Regional History Day Competition (Upper Shore Regional Event)  
- **Date of article:** March 31 2024 (event held March 23 2024)  
- **Location of event:** Chesapeake College, Wye Mills, MD (Eastern Shore)  
- **Organiser:** Thomas Hayman – teacher with Queen Anne’s County Public Schools (QACPS)  
- **Funding source:** Maryland Humanities (provides grant funding for the regional event)  
- **Venue contribution:** Chesapeake College donated the space for exhibits, presentations, and performances at no cost.  
- **Participating counties:** Caroline, Cecil, Queen Anne’s, and Talbot counties.  
- **Number of student participants:** *Nearly 200* middle‑ and high‑school students.  
- **Number of teachers/volunteers:** *30* area teachers and a “troop of volunteers.”  
- **Number of schools represented:** *Six* schools in total (Caroline County among them).  

#### Event Theme & Content  
- **Theme:** “Turning Points in History.”  
- **Project topics included:**  
  - Pop‑culture history  
  - Women’s rights  
  - The Great Depression  
  - Technological improvements  
  - Fast‑food’s historical impact  

#### Competition Structure  
- **Levels:**  
  1. **Local school‑level** competitions → winners advance to regional event.  
  2. **Regional (Upper Shore) event** → top two from each category per county advance to state competition.  
  3. **State competition** → top two per category advance to national competition.  

- **Categories of entries:**  
  - Paper (Individual)  
  - Website  
  - Performance  
  - Documentary  
  - Exhibit  

- **Divisions by grade:**  
  - **Youth Division:** 5th grade  
  - **Junior Division:** Grades 6‑8  
  - **Senior Division:** Grades 9‑12  

- **Awards presented (over 50 total):**  
  - Sports History Award  
  - Heritage Award  
  - Natural History Award  
  - Military History Award  
  - Contemporary World History Award  
  - African American History Award  
  - Colonial History Award  
  - Equality in History Award  
  - Medical History Award  
  - Chesapeake Bay/Maritime Award  
  - First, second, third place finishes and “Best in Region” awards per category  

#### Additional Event Details  
- **Food service:** Food trucks were on site for participants.  
- **Purpose:** To foster a love of history among students and develop research/presentation skills comparable to science contests.
