### Schools & Institutions  
- **Ridgely Elementary School** – Caroline County public elementary school.  
- **Ridgely Town Hall** – Municipal building where the parade begins; employees served as “haunted house” staff.

### Event Details  
- Annual Halloween parade held **Thursday, Oct 31 2024** (the day of the story).  
- Students marched down **Central Avenue** in costume.  
- **18 local businesses** participated, handing out candy and supporting the event.  
- Town Hall staff dressed as witches, fortune‑tellers, Headless Horseman, Beetlejuice, Ghostbusters, etc.

### Themes / Ongoing Issues  
- Strong collaboration between the town government, local businesses, and the school’s PTA.  
- Community involvement is highlighted as a long‑standing tradition (started early 2000s, expanded since 2011).

**Date:** September 23 2025  

### People & Roles

| (Unnamed) Caroline County Commissioners | County elected officials | Caroline County Government |
| (Unnamed) Maryland Family Network | Advocacy / Funding partner | Not‑for‑profit (mentioned) |

### Schools & Institutions / Programs  
- **Early Head Start** – Federal program serving pregnant women and children birth‑to‑age 3 in **Caroline and Cecil Counties**.  
- **Maryland Rural Development Cooperation (MRDC)** – Organization that runs Early Head Start in the two counties.  

### Budget / Funding Details  
- Program **ending effective Sept 2 2025** after failing to receive state funding.  
- MRDC had **over $1 million** in existing funding; had applied for **$3 million**.  
- Funding loss attributed to **applying as a direct recipient** rather than a sub‑recipient of Maryland Family Network.  

### Timeline & Key Dates  
- Funding cut announced **Aug 27 2025**.  
- Program termination **Sept 2 2025**.  

### Options & Alternatives Discussed  
- **Patty Center model** – Integrated services (e.g., resume building, job search) with childcare on‑site.  
- **Fee‑structure option** – Explore fees residents could pay to keep a center open in **Greensboro** or **Federalsburg**.  
- **Conversion of existing Head Start spots** – Collaboration with Maryland State Department of Education for classroom models.
