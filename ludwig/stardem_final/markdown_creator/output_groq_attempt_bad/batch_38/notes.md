## Story 10 – “Caroline County Public Library celebrates renovation” (May 22 2024)

**Institution**
- **Caroline County Public Library** – Denton, Caroline County  

**Key Personnel**
- **Nancy Pollen** – Library associate  
- **Connie Kumor** – Architect, RMM Architects (project architect)  
- **Debbie Bennett** – Executive Director (retiring end of June 2024)  
- **Irene Padilla** – State Librarian (Maryland)  

**Facility Enhancements & Programs**
- **Teen zone** – Gaming stations  
- **Adult area** – Study rooms, Zoom meeting capability for tutoring  
- **Children’s area** – New open layout, removal of a central dividing wall, added family restrooms, improved lighting, puppet programs, “read to a beagle” sessions  
- General “makerspace,” computer access, internet, printing services  

**Funding & Financial Details**
- **Maryland State Library Capital Grant** – (amount not specified)  
- **Friends of the Library** fundraising goal: **$50,000**; actual raised: **$78,000** (large donations + hundreds of small donations)  
- Additional “large donations” and “political support” from state legislators mentioned (no amounts given)  

**Renovation Timeline & Scope**
- Planning began **FY 2019** (space utilization study, design plan).  
- Renovation work focused on interior: first‑floor remodel, replacement of slate on the back pitched roof, major elevator upgrade.  
- Original building footprint unchanged.  

**Historical Context**
- Library founded by **John Hargreaves** in **1961**.  

**Quotes**
- *Nancy Pollen*:  
  > “We have got gaming stations in the teen zone and for adults we have study rooms and zoom meetings for tutoring.”  
  > “We make it so much fun. We have puppet programs and read to a beagle.”  

- *Connie Kumor*:  
  > “A lot of the design was making the space more friendly and open.”  
  > “When we started the project, especially the children's area, there was a big dividing wall right down the middle, and there were no restrooms for families to use while they were in this space, and the lighting levels were a bit tough for a library environment. We really wanted to open up the space for everybody and make it more inviting.”  

- *Debbie Bennett*:  
  > “This is terribly exciting.”  
  > “We started in the fiscal year of 2019. We did a space utilization study and a design plan, then the renovation of the first floor, finally the replacement of the slate on the back pitched roof and a major upgrade on the elevator.”  

- *Irene Padilla*:  
  > “We know what a great resource this is for Caroline County.
