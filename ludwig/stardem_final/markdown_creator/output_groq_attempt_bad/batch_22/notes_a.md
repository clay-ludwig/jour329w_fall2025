## Story 2 – Board of Ed selects North Caroline site for new Lockerman Middle (August 5 2025)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Date of board decision** | August 5 2025 (Caroline County Board of Education meeting) |
| **Location of meeting** | Denton, Caroline County, MD |
| **Decision** | Unanimous vote to locate the new Lockerman Middle School on the North Caroline High School site (adjacent to the high school) |
| **Site size** | **15.52 acres** allocated for the new middle‑school building |
| **Key individuals** | **Robyn Toth** – Architect for the new school (quoted on infrastructure, site readiness, mentorship opportunities, shared facilities, event space, no street crossings). <br>
