## Story 3 – “Denton collegian earns Meoli scholarship” (2023‑11‑01)

### People
- **Faith Johnson** – Resident of Denton, MD (Caroline County).  
  - Graduate of **North Caroline High School**.  
  - Played defense for six years on North Caroline’s field hockey team.  
  - Began working in 2021 at **McDonald’s in Denton**.  
  - Currently attending **Penn State University** (State College) studying **veterinary medicine**.  

- **Michael A. Meoli** – Owner and operator of **The Meoli Family McDonald’s Restaurants**; spokesperson for Meoli Companies.  

- (Other scholarship recipients listed are from Delaware and are not related to Caroline County.)

### Schools / Institutions / Organizations
- **North Caroline High School** – Public high school located in Caroline County, MD.  
- **Meoli Companies** – Parent company of The Meoli Family McDonald’s Restaurants; headquartered in **Rehoboth Beach, DE**; operates **25 McDonald’s restaurants** across the Delmarva Peninsula.  
- **McDonald’s (Denton location)** – Workplace where Faith Johnson was employed.  

### Program / Initiative
- **Meoli Companies Scholarship Program**  
  - Total scholarship fund: **$10,000** (awarded to five recent high‑school graduates who are current or former Meoli employees).  
  - Selection criteria (as stated): demonstrated **leadership**, **participation in local community activities**, **work experience**, **overcoming challenges**, and **inspiration**.
