## Story 2 – Cellphone pouches pilot (Caroline County)

- **Date published:** August 29 2025  
- **Location:** Caroline County (middle schools)  

### People
| Name | Title / Role | Organization |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| Derek Simmons | Superintendent | Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS) |

### Programs / Initiatives
- **Cellphone‑pouch pilot** (second year, middle schools)  
  - Introduced 2024‑25 school year as a pilot to keep students off phones.  
  - Uses locked magnetic pouches (company **Yondr**) that travel with each student.  
  - Phones are locked at the start of the day, unlocked at day’s end with a magnetic device.  
  - If a pouch is left at home, the phone is turned over to the school office for the day.  
  - Planned expansion: after another year of middle‑school data, district will consider moving the program to high schools.

### Results (Year 1)
- **30.6 % fewer referrals** than the prior year.  
- **130 fewer attacks, threats, or fights**.  
- **≈200 fewer instances** of disrespect and disruptions.

### Governance
- Statistics presented at the **June Caroline County Board of Education meeting**.  
- Board accepted the recommendation to continue the program for another year at middle schools.
