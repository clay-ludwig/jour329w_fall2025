### Program Details & Timeline  
| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Program duration (2023)** | 5 weeks, June 23 – July 27 (19‑day schedule). |
| **Total participants** | 916 students (747 elementary, 169 secondary). |
| **Elementary attendance** | Avg. 14 days out of 19. |
| **Multilingual learners** | 70 total; 49 at Greensboro Elementary. |
| **Middle‑school enrollment** | 40 students (goal to boost middle‑school program). |
| **High‑school credit‑recovery** | 131 participants (92 North Caroline, 39 Colonel Richardson). |
| **Students with disabilities** | 127 total (102 elementary, 25 secondary) – increase of 30 from prior year. |
| **Meals served** | ~15,000 meals (breakfast + lunch) by CCPS Food Service. |
| **Health services** | Nurses & health assistants staffed health rooms; School‑Based Wellness Centers open. |
| **CTE component** | 45‑minute daily CTE mini‑sessions for middle‑schoolers; aligns with Blueprint for Maryland’s Future. |
| **Future goal** | Increase enrollment by ~400 students → target 1,200–1,300 participants next summer. |
| **Funding sources (2023)** | • American Rescue Plan – Elementary & Secondary School Emergency Relief (ARP ESSER) Fund  <br>• Food services revenue  <br>• In‑kind use of school buildings  <br>• Pending 21st Century Grant. |
| **Grant/partner outreach** | Superintendent Simmons: pursuing additional grants, potential YMCA partnership, continued collaboration with Recreation & Parks. |
