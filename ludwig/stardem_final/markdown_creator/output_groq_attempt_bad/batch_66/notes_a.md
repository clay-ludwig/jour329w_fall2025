### Story 1 – “State test scores: Local districts increase in English and math, dip in science” (August 27 2024)

**Date of publication:** August 27, 2024  
**Source:** *The Star Democrat* (Easton, MD) – Local News section  

#### Schools / Districts Mentioned
- **Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS)** – the public school district serving Caroline County, MD.

#### Test Scores & Rankings (2023‑2024 Maryland Comprehensive Assessment Program)
| Subject | Change vs. 2022‑2023 | 2023‑2024 Proficiency % | Statewide Rank (out of 24 districts) |
|---------|----------------------|-----------------------|----------------------------------------|
| English (Reading) | **+2.1 pp** | **45.3 %** | **15th** |
| Math | **+3.9 pp** | **19.7 %** | **15th** |
| 5th‑grade Science | **–17.4 pp** (34.4 % → 17 %) | – | **13th → 18th** |
| 8th‑grade Science | **–2.8 pp** (22.1 % → 19.3 %) | – | **15th → 17th** |

*(“pp” = percentage points)*  

#### Statewide Context (for comparison)
- Maryland Statewide English proficiency: **48.4 %**  
- Maryland Statewide Math proficiency: **24.1 %**  
- Statewide increases (2023‑2024 vs. 2022‑2023): **+0.5 pp** in English, **+0.8 pp** in Math.

#### Agencies / Programs Referenced
- **Maryland State Department of Education** – released the test‑score data.  
- **Maryland Comprehensive Assessment Program (MCAP)** – the assessment system used for the reported scores.  
- **Maryland State Board of Education** – held the meeting where results were presented.

#### Ongoing Issues / Themes (as reported for Caroline County)
- **Improvement in English and Math** – modest gains above state averages, but still below the statewide proficiency percentages.  
- **Decline in Science proficiency** – notable drops in both 5th‑ and 8th‑grade science, with corresponding falls in statewide rankings.  
- **Ranking Position** – Caroline County sits in the middle of the pack (15th) for English and Math, but slipped further in science rankings.
