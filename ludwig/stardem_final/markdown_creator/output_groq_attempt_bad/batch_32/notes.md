It’s important for our students to hear that it’s OK to try hard and come up short; in all good design thinking, failure is just part of the learning process.”  

### Ongoing Issues / Themes  
- Emphasis on STEM exposure, design‑thinking, and perseverance for elementary students.

## Story 9 – School district saves thousands in energy costs – Program focuses on use and use reduction  
**Date:** August 9 2023  

### People  
| Name | Title / Role | Affiliation |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| Scott Muir | Client Manager | Cenergistic (energy‑conservation company) |
| Dr. Derek Simmons | Superintendent | Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS) |
| Richard Barton | Board President | Caroline County Board of Education |
| Josh Rein | Energy Specialist | Cenergistic (assigned to CCPS) |
| (Author) Connie Connolly | Reporter | — |

### Schools / Institutions / Organizations  
- **Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS)** – K‑12 public school system in Caroline County.  
- **Cenergistic** – Energy‑conservation firm based in Dallas, Texas; contracted for a five‑year energy‑savings program with CCPS.  
- **Caroline County Board of Education** – Governing body for CCPS.  

### Budget / Financial Figures  
- **Total savings:** Approximately **$260,000** (reported as “almost $260,000”) since program start **July 1 2022**.  
- **Energy cost increases (baseline):**  
  - Electricity: **+64 %** increase.  
  - Heating oil: **+42 %** increase.  
- **Energy Use Intensity (EUI):**  
  - Prior to program: **≈ 50 EUI** (EPA benchmark).  
  - After one year: **32 EUI** (down 35 %).  
- **Building audits performed:** **1,516** audits.  
- **CO₂ emissions avoided:** **1,451 metric tons** (equivalent to > 3.5 million vehicle miles, enough to power **175 homes** for a year).  

### Dates & Timeline  
- **Program start:** **July 1 2022**.  
- **Annual report presented:** **August 1 2023** at the Caroline County Board of Education meeting.  
- **Energy‑savings “Day”:** **July 3 2023** (initiative highlighted).  

### Programs / Initiatives / Policies  
- **Cenergistic Energy‑Conservation Program** – focuses on “use and use reduction” across four energy sources: electricity, solar, heating oil, propane.  
- **Energy Use Intensity (EUI) tracking** – benchmarked against EPA standards; goal of Energy Star certification for facilities.  
- **Solar utilization & thermostat setbacks** – part of cost‑reduction tactics.  

### Contact Information (when available)  
- No direct phone numbers or email addresses provided in the article.  

### Quotes  
- **Scott Muir (Cenergistic Client Manager):** “This is one of the fastest and most successful starts to a program that I’ve seen.”  
- **Richard Barton (Board President):** “I’ve just got to give you guys big kudos… really exciting stuff.”  
- **Scott Muir (on CO₂ avoidance):** “That equates to over 3.5 million miles driven by an average car… It’s a huge environmental footprint.”  

### Ongoing Issues / Themes  
- Managing rising utility costs (especially electricity) while maintaining school budgets.  
- Reducing district’s carbon footprint and improving energy efficiency.  
- Pursuing Energy Star certification for school facilities.
