### Project Overview (Technical Center at Chesapeake College)
- **Project:** New Technical Center (Technical Center) to be built at Chesapeake College.  
- **Total Estimated Cost:** **$79 million**.  
- **Funding Split:**  
  - **State of Maryland:** 75% of total cost.  
  - **Local Counties:** Remaining 25% to be provided collectively by the participating counties (each county’s share to be determined through its own appropriations).  
- **Participating Counties:** Caroline, Dorchester, Kent, Talbot, and Queen Anne’s.  
- **Decision Deadline for Counties:** Submit **Letter of Intent** to the Maryland Higher Education Commission **by September 28, 2023** (ahead of a meeting at the Department of Budget and Management).  
- **Construction Timeline:** Set for **2026**.  
- **Location:** Adjacent to the **Eastern Shore Higher Education Center**.  

### Facility Specifications
- **Total Size:** **> 90,000 sq ft**.  
- **Assignable Space:** **52,000 sq ft** (portion of the total that can be assigned to programs).  

### Programs / Trades to be Housed (as listed)
- Electrical  
- Welding  
- HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air‑Conditioning)  
- Culinary  
- Skilled Office/Administrative Support  
- Marine Trades  
- Construction Trades Training  

### Funding & Equipment Details
- **State Funding:** Covers 75% of the capital project cost.  
- **Local Funding Requirement:** Each county that signs a Letter of Intent must pass appropriations to cover its share of the remaining 25%.  
- **Equipment Costs:** Not included in the $79 M estimate; equipment will be moved from existing facilities or funded later through private funding, public grants, etc. (as explained by Chesapeake College President **Cliff Coppersmith**).  

### Key Quotes (non‑Caroline officials, retained for context)
- **Cliff Coppersmith (President, Chesapeake College):**  
  - “Our problem right now is we’ve got so much equipment crammed in these spaces, there’s not a lot of room for students.” (referring to the current Queen Anne’s Technical Building).  
  - “The estimated cost includes furnishing the building. The equipment is not a part of that estimate.”  
- **Kent County Commission President Ron Fithian:** Highlighted overall county concerns about the financial aspect of the project.  
- **Other County Officials:** Various statements about fiscal responsibilities and support (Dorchester, Queen Anne’s, Talbot) — not directly related to Caroline County but part of the collaborative discussion.

### procedural notes
- **Letter of Intent:** Formal expression of each county’s willingness to contribute its local share; must be submitted before the Sept 28 deadline.  
- **Appropriations:** After letters are submitted, each county’s governing body must pass the necessary budget appropriations to fund its portion of the local share.
