**Story 1 – Caroline Commissioners approve summer school grant (Nov 29 2023)**  

- **Grant source & amount** – Maryland Department of Education awarded a **three‑year, $1.2 million grant** to fund Caroline County summer learning centers.  
- **Presentation** – Jamie Beachy, **Director of Caroline County Recreation and Parks**, presented the grant to the County Commissioners.  
- **Planned summer‑year enrollment** –  
  - **First summer year**: **5 school sites** serving **1,100 students** (up from 700 students in prior years).  
  - **Following two years (2025‑2026)**: Planned to serve **3 elementary schools** with a total of **400 students** *unless* additional funding is secured.  
- **Commissioners present** –  
  - **Travis Breeding**, President of the Caroline County Board of Commissioners  
  - **Larry Porter**, Vice President of the Board of Commissioners  
  - **Other commissioners** (names not listed) voted to accept the grant.  
- **Other county business (non‑education)** – $1,011,834 contract with Wellpath LLC for jail medical services; sale of two surplus vehicles; personnel change in Public Works.  
- **Meeting details** –  
  - Date of meeting where grant was discussed: **Tuesday, Nov. 21 2023** (9 a.m.) at the Caroline County Courthouse, Denton.  
  - Next regular commission meeting: **Tuesday, Dec. 5 2023** (9 a.m.) in Room 106, Caroline County Courthouse, 109 Market St., Denton.  
  - Public attendance: meetings are held **9 a.m. Tuesdays** (except the fifth Tuesday) and are open to the public, with security screening.  
  - Remote participation: Zoom link **https://zoom.us/j/300062187**; toll‑free dial‑in numbers **888‑475‑4499** or **877‑853‑5257**; Meeting ID **300 062 187**.
