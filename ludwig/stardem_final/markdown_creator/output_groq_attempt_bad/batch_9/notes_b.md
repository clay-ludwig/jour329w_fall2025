### Schools & Institutions
- **Caroline County Board of Education** – Governing body overseeing public schools in the county.
- **Colonel Richardson High School** – One of the two high schools that selects a student board representative; Vanessa Odidika’s school.
- **North Caroline High School** – The other high school that selects a student board representative; Kadeen Belle’s school.
- **Elementary & Middle Schools in Caroline County** – Student reps are expected to gather information about activities/events at these schools (specific schools not named).

### Student Representative Details
- **Selection Process**: Each year both Caroline County high schools select one student to serve as a non‑voting board representative; the board maintains a policy of having **two** such representatives.
- **Responsibilities**:  
  - Provide information about activities and events occurring at their own high school **and** at elementary and middle schools in the county.  
  - Attend board meetings and contribute a student voice (non‑voting).
- **Start Date**: Representatives begin duties in **September** (2024).
