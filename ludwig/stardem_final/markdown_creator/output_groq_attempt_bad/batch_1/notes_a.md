### Orientation & Onboarding Details
- **Date of breakfast opening:** Tuesday, Aug 15, 2023.  
- **Orientation length:** Six‑day program, concluding Thursday, Aug 24, 2023.  
- **Number of new teachers welcomed:** **55** (for the 2023‑2024 school year).  
- **Training focus areas:** Instruction, technology, safety & security, communication, data systems.  
- **Bus tour route:** From **Marydel** to **Federalsburg**, covering all ten CCPS schools.  
- **Tour logistics:** Buses driven by Superintendent Dr. Derek Simmons and driver Leonard Buckle; tour guides Rob Willoughby (HR) & Garrett Shull (Transportation).  
- **Key stop topics:**  
  - **Support Services Center:** “Breakfast in the Classroom” program and “Backpack Program.”  
  - **Caroline County Culinary Center:** Lunch for participants.  
- **Final day activities (Aug 24 at Colonel Richardson High School):**  
  - Presentation by Dr. Derek Simmons.  
  - Professional‑development sessions with mentor teachers.  
  - Resource fair.  
  - Lunch provided by **Mission BBQ**.  
  - Ice‑cream and yard games on the campus lawn.
