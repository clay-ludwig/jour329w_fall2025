## Story 2 – Caroline County school athletic facilities will soon have bathrooms  

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Schools involved** | • **North Caroline High School** (outdoor athletic facilities)<br>• **Colonel Richardson High School** (outdoor athletic facilities) |
| **County officials** | • **Travis Breeding** – President, Caroline County Board of Commissioners<br>• **Bill Mengel** – Director of Operations, Caroline County Public Schools |
| **School district official** | **Derek Simmons** – Superintendent, Caroline County Public Schools |
| **Public‑works official** | **Robin Eaton** – Public Works Director, Caroline County |
| **Budget** | Included in the **Caroline County Fiscal Year 2025 budget** (exact dollar amount not disclosed). |
| **Project start** | Initiated **March 2024** (when commissioners and school officials began prioritizing the bathrooms). |
| **Facilities details** | • **North Caroline High School** – Separate men’s and women’s sides, **three toilets each**.<br>• **Colonel Richardson High School** – Separate men’s and women’s sides, **two toilets each**.<br>• Locations: Near the baseball and football fields at each high school, serving fall, winter and spring sports. |
| **Projected completion** | Expected to be **operational by late September or early October 2025**. |
| **Quotes** | • **Travis Breeding**: “We had noticed … we only had Porta‑Johns, and we didn’t have any place for people to wash their hands or you get out of colder temperatures … we wanted to make sure that our students, parent and visitor attendees at sporting events had good bathing facilities to use at our high schools.”<br>• **Derek Simmons**: “The restrooms will *greatly enhance the experience for students and community members alike as they support our athletic events*.”<br>• **Robin Eaton**: “*To have a bathroom in itself is great*.” |
