## Story 1 – “Girls lose gains in math and science post‑pandemic” (Sept. 17 2025)

### People, Titles & Roles
- **Mark DeMorra** – Maryland’s 4‑H STEM specialist; quoted on cyclical nature of education trends.  
- **District representatives** – Unnamed representatives from Caroline County Public Schools who requested specific questions from the Star Democrat (questions were provided but not answered).  

### Schools, Institutions & Organizations
- **Caroline County Public Schools** – The district for Caroline County, Maryland; did not respond to the Star Democrat’s request for comment.  
- **Caroline County (Mid‑Shore district)** – One of the three Mid‑Shore counties (Talbot, Caroline, Dorchester) referenced in the study.  

### Data & Numbers (Math NAEP Scores, 2019‑2024)
| Metric | Caroline County |
|--------|-----------------|
| **2023‑24 Girls’ average score** | 4.51 |
| **2023‑24 Boys’ average score** | 4.44 |
| **Girls’ decline (2019‑2024)** | **1.18 points** (≈ one full grade level) |
| **Boys’ decline (2019‑2024)** | **0.81 points** |
| **Statewide (Maryland) girls’ decline** | 0.92 points |
| **Statewide (Maryland) boys’ decline** | 0.47 points |

- **Score context**: NAEP scores are on a scale where 5.5 equals the 2019 national average; each whole point equals one grade‑level equivalent.  

### Study Details
- **Source**: Stanford Education Data Archive & National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) analysis (cited via Associated Press).  
- **Comparison**: 2023 NAEP results versus 2019 national average, covering grades 3‑8.  
- **Finding**: No Maryland districts have fully recovered to pre‑2019 math levels (per Stanford Education Data Archive).  

### District Interaction with Media
- The Star Democrat provided the data to **Caroline County Public Schools** (and Talbot County Public Schools) two weeks before publication.  
- **Caroline County** did not issue a comment; district representatives asked for specific questions, which were supplied but left unanswered.  

### Quotes (Caroline‑specific context)
- **Mark DeMorra (4‑H STEM specialist)**:  
  > “There’s a lot of cyclical things happening in education… It’s kind of like a pendulum in the fact that we swung so far to one side.”

### Themes & Ongoing Issues (Caroline County)
- **Significant gender‑based math score decline** – Girls in Caroline County lost **1.18 points**, a larger drop than the state average for girls.  
- **Lack of district response** – Indicates possible communication gap or reluctance to comment on the data.  
- **Broader state trend** – Caroline County’s decline mirrors a statewide pattern where girls’ math performance has fallen more sharply than boys’.
