## STORY 5 – Caroline County Education Foundation Launched (Nov. 22 2023)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Date of article** | November 22, 2023 |
| **Byline** | Connie Connolly – cconnolly@chespub.com |
| **Foundation name** | **Caroline County Education Foundation** (sub‑fund of the Mid‑Shore Community Foundation) |
| **Launch date** | October 5 (2023) |
| **Key leaders (founders)** | • **Tolbert Rowe** – Vice President, Bay Capital Mortgage (Annapolis) <br>• **Robert Willey** – Certified Public Accountant, Denton <br>• **Dr. James Orr Jr.** <br>• **Wayne Cole** – Chief Financial Officer, WIN Transport, Inc. (Federalsburg) |
| **Superintendent quote** | **Dr. Derek Simmons** – “Now we truly do have an education foundation in Caroline County… financial literacy is a massive undertaking and needed.” |
| **Historical foundation** | • Established 1987, operated ~10 years, mini‑grant system (max **$500** per grant) → dissolved mid‑1990s. <br>• Remaining **$8,000** in legacy account (still overseen by Willey). |
| **Current focus** | **Financial literacy** for K‑12 students; career exploration; support for Junior Achievement programs. |
| **Partnerships** | • **Junior Achievement of the Eastern Shore** – memorandum of understanding; upcoming 25,000‑sq‑ft regional facility in Salisbury (students from Caroline County have the greatest distance and poverty, requiring travel support). |
| **Blueprint for Maryland Education** | • Requires career exploration, dual enrollment, and financial‑literacy components. <br>• Caroline County is the only system in the state requiring a full high‑school credit in finance for graduation. |
| **Funding & budget notes** | • No specific new budget amount disclosed for the foundation. <br>• Mention of Blueprint funding gaps: “The math in Blueprint doesn’t add up… They did not take financial literacy.” |
| **Contact/organization info** | Mid‑Shore Community Foundation (parent organization); foundation to adopt bylaws soon; seeking additional board members. |
