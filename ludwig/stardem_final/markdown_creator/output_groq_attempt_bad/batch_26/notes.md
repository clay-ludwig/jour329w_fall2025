## Story 8 – “Talbot County pledges support for Chesapeake College project” (Jul 25 2024)

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Project** | **Queen Anne’s Center Technical** facility at **Chesapeake College – Wye Mills campus** (supports trade programs). |
| **Total Estimated Cost** | **~$56 million** |
| **State Funding Expected** | **~$42 million** (≈ 75 % of total). |
