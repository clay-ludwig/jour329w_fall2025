## Story 6 – “Done for the children” (June 9 2024)

**Program / Initiative**  
- **Read to Me Program** – offers one‑on‑one reading sessions with a trained therapy canine and handler for children who are struggling to read (for any reason).  
- **Location:** Caroline County Library (specific branch not named).  

**Key Details**  
- Therapy dogs are **female Rottweilers**; all four owned by the author have been approved by the **Alliance of Therapy Dogs** and **Crisis Response Canines**.  
- **Requirements for therapy teams:** background check for both dog and handler; must pass handling tests; dogs must be desensitized to loud noises, yelling, falling, sirens, etc.  
- The author reports **> 1,000 visits** with the dogs and **national recognition** for their therapy work.  
- Planned visits for the 2023‑2024 school year: **seven classrooms at Easton Elementary School** (Sept‑Dec session).  
- A teacher’s letter later stated **“no dogs were allowed at school”** due to a “policy change”; the author has not received further updates.  

**Stakeholder / Quote**  
> “I really enjoyed the article in Sunday's Star Democrat, about the Read to Me Program, at Caroline County Library. Children struggling to read (for any reason), can request a session with a canine and handler at the library. It is proven that canines open many doors for children struggling emotionally and physically. Children feel comfortable with a canine. Canines break down barriers, do not judge, are fluffy, motivate and create trust.” – **Barbara Lee Jefferson**, Easton (author of the letter).  

**Contact Information**  
- No direct contact details for the Read to Me Program or Caroline County Library were provided in the story.
