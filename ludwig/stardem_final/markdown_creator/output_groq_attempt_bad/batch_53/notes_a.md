## STORY 1 – “Caroline Commissioners hear update on potential for new middle school” (Nov 27 2024)

### People & Roles
| Person | Title / Role | Affiliation |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Bill Mengel** | Director of Operations | Caroline County Public Schools |
| **Travis Breeding** | President (Commission President) | Caroline County Board of Commissioners |
| **Three Commissioners** (unnamed) | County Commissioners | Caroline County |

### Schools & Facilities Mentioned
- **Colonel Richardson Middle School** – existing middle school in Federalsburg (Caroline County).
- **Lockerman Middle School** – existing middle school in Denton (Caroline County).
- **Denton Elementary School** – site of proposed $500,000 HVAC work.

### Organizations & Agencies
- **Caroline County Public Schools** (CCPS)
- **Maryland Interagency Commission on School Construction** (state agency reviewing the funding request)

### Budget Figures & Financial Details
| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| HVAC work at Denton Elementary School | **$500,000** | Top priority request in revised capital improvement plan |
| Early planning & design costs for new/expanded middle school (FY 2026‑27 & FY 2027‑28) | **$5.5 million** | Top priority request for years 1‑2 |
| Requested state funding for new middle school construction | **$76 million** | Represents ~89 % of total estimated cost |
| Total estimated cost for years 1‑2 (state + county) | **$86 million** | County responsible for ~11 % (~$10 million) |
| Approximate square‑footage being considered for new build | **126,000 sq ft** | Per Maryland Interagency Commission on School Construction |
| Projected construction cost per square foot (if shovels in FY 28, occupancy FY 30) | **≈ $490/sq ft** | Driver of overall cost |

### Timeline & Process
- **Planning Phase**: Transitioned from “talking phase” (Aug‑Sept 2024) to “planning phase” (Nov 2024).  
- **Feasibility Study**: To be commissioned; expected completion **6‑8 months** after start.  
- **Design & Construction**: Estimated **≈ 5 years** total from start to occupancy.  
- **Potential Build Schedule**: Shovels in **FY 28**, occupancy for **FY 30 school year**.

### Key Quotes
- *Bill Mengel*:  
  > “One thing I've learned about building a new school is that it's a very serious endeavor. It takes a very long time to build one, approximately five years. But things change very, very quickly. That's really what kind of happened here, early October.”  
- *Bill Mengel* (on square footage):  
  > “How do we get more square footage?” (stated as the district’s main point of negotiation)  
- *Travis Breeding*:  
  > “Keep us in the loop on the study, because we would like to, if we're on the hook for 11% of this, we'd like to have input into, is it a renovation, addition, new location, all those discussions.”  
- *Bill Mengel* (on enrollment trends):  
  > “I am aware, and so is the Board of Education, that there are some communities that are planned in and around Denton and points north (in) Caroline County. We expect that there will be some students moving in impacting our populations. The last thing I want to do a build such as this, and then have it be at 100% occupancy within a couple of years.”

### Decisions & Actions
- **Revised Capital Improvement Plan**: Unanimously approved by the three commissioners on **Nov 27 2024**.  
- **Top Priority Funding Requests**: $500k HVAC (Denton Elementary) and $5.5 M early planning/design for new/expanded middle school.  
- **State Funding Request**: $76 M submitted to Maryland Interagency Commission on School Construction for a potential new middle school.

### Ongoing Issues & Themes
- Determining **whether to build a new middle school, renovate an existing one, or add on** – contingent on feasibility study outcomes and Board of Education approval.  
- **Financing split**: County responsible for roughly 11 % of total project cost; desire for input on cost‑share decisions.  
- **Projected enrollment growth** in Denton and northern Caroline County areas influencing facility planning.  
- **Cost escalation**: Rising construction cost per square foot driving budget considerations.
