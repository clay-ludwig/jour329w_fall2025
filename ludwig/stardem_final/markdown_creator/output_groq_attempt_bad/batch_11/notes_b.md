## Caroline County Education Notes (Batch 11)

### Story 2 – “No primary election to be held for Caroline County Board of Education seats” (April 24, 2024)

- **Election Details**
  - The Caroline County Board of Education election for **District 2** will **skip the May 14 primary** and go straight to the **November 5, 2024 General Election** ballot.
  - Reason: **Fewer than three candidates** filed for the race.
  - The contest will appear on the **Caroline County ballot** alongside state and federal offices.

- **Candidates**
  - **Stefanie Johnson** – sole candidate for District 2.
    - Runs under the committee name **“Stefanie Johnson for Caroline BOE.”**

- **Board of Education Seats**
  - **Two** Board of Education seats are up for election in Caroline County in 2024.

- **Voting Options**
  - Caroline County residents may vote **in person during early voting**, on **Election Day**, or **by mail** for the November 5, 2024 election.
