### Programs, Initiatives & Projects
- **MAEOE Green School Certification**  
  - Two‑year application process requiring evidence of meeting sub‑category objectives and completion of a prescribed curriculum.  
  - Schools must provide at least **one example of outdoor or environmental instruction per grade level**.

- **Denton Elementary – Breakfast Bag Recycling Project**  
  - Third‑grade students construct recycling boxes with signs.  
  - Goal: divert ~650 plastic breakfast bags (one per student) from trash each morning.

- **Greensboro Elementary – Garden & Sustainability Projects**  
  - **Garden Tower**: strawberries & herbs.  
  - **Pollinator Garden**.  
  - **Environmental Club**: planting native plants in swales; planned native‑plant courtyard installation before end of school year.  
  - Secured multiple grants for future projects (e.g., tree planting).

### Direct Quotes
- *Julie Strange* (Denton Elementary):  
  > “You have to work together, and we divided and conquered… It’s an intense process, but again, eye‑opening how helpful everybody was and that there are people for everything.”  
  > “Every day, we get breakfast in plastic bags per child, and our school is about 650 kids… The kids were so excited about it, and they did it… the kids are responsible for putting their bag in there when they’re done their breakfast instead of throwing it in the trash.”

- *Zellie Kneller Kidigarden* (Greensboro Elementary):  
  > “Through our curriculum and standards, we’re able to teach these kids sustainability practices and how to take care of the Earth… It’s one of my passions. I really enjoy doing it.”  
  > “I’m so grateful that we’re a community school because being a community school allows us to work with… stakeholders in the community, and we get wonderful things from all the members in the community.”  
  > “It’s really, really, really important work and every school should be Green School certified. It is life‑changing for a lot of students and adults… because this is the planet that we live on and everyone needs to learn how to take care of it because we don’t have anywhere else to go.”
