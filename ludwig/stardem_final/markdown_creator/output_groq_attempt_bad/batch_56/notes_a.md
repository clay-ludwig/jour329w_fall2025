## Story 3 – “Lockerman High School celebrates 95th anniversary” (July 29 2025)

**Key Individuals**
- **Willie Woods** – Chairperson, Lockerman Schools Association Inc.  
  - Quote: “It was very gratifying to see the community continuing to just work together and come together to celebrate the rich history that Lockerman has and the role it has played in the lives of multiple generations of residents of Caroline County.”
- **George W. Fisher** – President, Caroline County Board of Education (1993 pre‑dedication ceremony).  
- **William D. Brown** – President, Concerned Friends of Lockerman (forerunner of the current Lockerman Schools Association Inc.).  

**Schools / Institutions / Organizations**
- **Lockerman High School** (originally Joseph H. Lockerman High School) – located in Denton, Caroline County.  
- **Lockerman Schools Association Inc.** – nonprofit established 1993; mission: “engage in educational, literary or charitable activities that benefit students who have ties to any of the Lockerman Schools.”  
- **Caroline County Board of Education** – attended the anniversary event.  

**Historical Timeline & Dates**
- **1930** – School founded; land donated by Isaac Thomas to the Black Parent‑Teachers Association.  
- **1931** – Named for Joseph H. Lockerman (born 1862 near Denton).  
- **1966** – Closed as a Black high school; became the integrated **Riverview Middle School**.  
- **1993** – “Lockerman” name restored; pre‑dedication ceremony held; Lockerman Schools Association Inc. incorporated.  
- **July 29 2025** – 95th anniversary celebration (theme: “Capturing our history, preserving our legacy and advancing our future”).  

**Event Details**
- Theme: “Capturing our history, preserving our legacy and advancing our future.”  
- Attendees included members of graduating classes **1935, 1945, 1955, 1965**; some absent members had family accept plaques/trophies on their behalf.  
- Caroline County officials and Board of Education representatives were present.  
- **13 scholarships** awarded by the Lockerman Schools Association Inc. to Caroline County students pursuing higher education.  

**Programs / Initiatives**
- Scholarship program (13 scholarships awarded in 2025).  

**Quotes (officials / stakeholders)**
- Willie Woods (Chairperson, Lockerman Schools Association Inc.):  
  > “It was very gratifying to see the community continuing to just work together and come together to celebrate the rich history that Lockerman has and the role it has played in the lives of multiple generations of residents of Caroline County.”
- Willie Woods on planning process:  
  > “You sort of share the vision, the idea, brainstorm with individuals and then begin to establish … It’s a working plan… Then we look at some things we want to recognize and honor…”
