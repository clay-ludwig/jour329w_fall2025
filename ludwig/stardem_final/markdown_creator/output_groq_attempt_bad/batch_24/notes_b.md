### Schools & Institutions
- **Caroline County Public Schools (CCPS)**
- **North Caroline High School** – location of Teacher of the Year.
- **Greensboro Elementary School** – location of Support Staff of the Year.
- **Cow Barn Events** – venue in Ridgely, Caroline County, for the award ceremony.

### Dates & Events
- **April 11 2025** – Annual award ceremony held at Cow Barn Events, Ridgely.
- **State Teacher of the Year contest** – upcoming; Caroline County will be represented by Andrew Buffenmyer (exact contest date not stated).

### Programs & Initiatives
- **Annual CCPS Award Ceremony** – honors “Teacher of the Year” and “Support Staff of the Year.”
- **State Teacher of the Year Contest** – statewide competition involving 24 Maryland school districts; Caroline County will have a representative.

### Quotes (verbatim)
- *Derek Simmons:* “We can't do the work without great people and great employees, and I'm glad we have a way to recognize them, not just our teachers, but especially our support staff.”
- *Andrew Buffenmyer:* “It’s an absolutely amazing feeling…students are still number one. The best thing is seeing those kids walk across that stage, seeing them in the community, seeing them be successful in their life, like that’s the real reward.”
- *Andrew Buffenmyer:* “(The award) is vindication for the hours, the tears, the long nights, the everything that we’ve put in behind the scenes… but it doesn’t change anything, like, students are still number one.”
- *Nick Marshall:* “I was surprised, a little shocked. I wasn’t expecting it being a fairly new employee of the county… I couldn’t ask for a better group of people to work with. The kids, seeing them every day and how they enjoy it, it’s just a pleasure.”
- *Nick Marshall:* “I appreciate it. I’ve told my supervisors that no matter what, this doesn’t change who I am. We don’t do this for awards. It’s for the school. It’s our job. It’s what we’re supposed to be doing.”
