## Story 5 – Eastern Shore legislators discuss education funding and county burdens  
**Date:** February 8 2025 **Author:** Konner Metz **Section:** Local News  

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Key individuals** | • **Hiram Burch** – Local government research and analysis manager, Department of Legislative Services  <br>• **Del. Steven Arentz** (R‑Kent, Queen Anne’s, Cecil, **Caroline**) – Commented on education funding sources  <br>• **Del. Sheree Sample‑Hughes** (D‑Dorchester, Wicomico) – Mentioned concerns about special‑education funding  <br>• **Del. Chris Adams** (R‑Caroline, Dorchester, Talbot, Wicomico) – Stated that the state will pass unfunded mandates to counties |
| **Organizations / agencies** | • Department of Legislative Services  <br>• Maryland Governor Wes Moore’s administration  <br>• Maryland State Legislature |
| **Budget / financial figures** | • State deficit: **$3 billion**  <br>• Proposed state‑level gap to be absorbed by local governments: **$144 million** (FY 2026)  <br>• Projected local cost shift for **Caroline County** (and Talbot County): **≈ $700,000**  <br>• Other Eastern Shore counties: Dorchester ≈ $830,000; Queen Anne’s ≈ $970,000; Kent ≈ $300,000 |
| **Education‑related cost items** | • Teacher retirement funding  <br>• No reduction to special‑education funding (per Burch)  <br>• Reductions noted for collaborative learning, foundation program, compensatory aid, English‑language‑learner program (but not special education) |
| **Quotes** | • **Steven Arentz:** “The governor’s saying we have record funding to education… I’m wondering if that’s mandated from The Blueprint.”  <br>• **Sheree Sample‑Hughes:** “Constituents are concerned about special‑education funding amid around $200 million in proposed cuts to the state Developmental Disabilities Administration.”  <br>• **Chris Adams:** “What this means really for Eastern Shore citizens is their tax rates … are going up… That’s how we’re unfortunately going to be funding Maryland.” |
| **Key issues / recurring themes** | • State budget shortfall pushing cost‑shifts onto counties  <br>• Debate over whether increased education funding is from the governor’s actions or mandated by **The Blueprint for Maryland’s Future**  <br>• Assurance that special‑education funding will be “held harmless” despite other program cuts  <br>• Concern that unfunded mandates will raise local property taxes |
