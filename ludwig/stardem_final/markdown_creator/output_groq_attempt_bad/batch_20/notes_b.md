## Caroline County Education Content (Story 4)

### People & Roles
- **Sharon Pepukayi** – Superintendent (Talbot County Public Schools). *Quote:* “I have some students that feel like in high school, let's relinquish some of that independence and they should be able to have the phones.” (Note: Mentioned in context of Talbot County; no Caroline County official quoted.)

### Schools & Districts
- **Easton Middle School** – Implementing locked cell‑phone pouch policy since December 2024.
- **Caroline County school districts** – Mentioned as having begun cell‑phone pouch pilot programs earlier in the school year (specific schools not named).

### Organizations & Programs
- **Yondr** – Company supplying locked cell‑phone pouches and magnetic unlocking devices used in the pilot.
- **Cell‑phone pouch pilot program** – Adopted by Caroline County (along with Dorchester County) prior to Talbot County’s rollout; continued at Easton Middle School.

### Dates & Timeline
- **December 2024** – Start of the cell‑phone pouch policy at Easton Middle School (and earlier pilot in Caroline County).
- **April 5 2025** – Publication date of the article reporting on four‑month progress.

### Ongoing Issues / Themes
- **Student engagement & multitasking** – Policy aims to reduce phone use during instruction.
- **Parent concerns about safety and emergency access** – Addressed through Yondr’s “portable” unlocking devices.
- **Potential for policy expansion** – Talbot County may expand to other schools next school year; mixed opinions from student advisory board.

### Quotes (pertinent to Caroline County reference)
> “Talbot County followed suit of **Caroline** and **Dorchester** school districts, both of which began cell phone pouch pilot programs closer to the beginning of the school year.” – Article narrative (no direct speaker).
