### Program Details / Initiatives
- **Program Name:** Introduction to Careers in Healthcare (ITCH)  
- **Target Audience:** Middle‑school and high‑school students across Caroline, Dorchester, Kent, Queen Anne’s, and Talbot counties.  
- **Goals (as listed in the letter):**  
  1. Introduce health‑care careers early.  
  2. Support local students throughout their educational journey.  
  3. Build a skilled, home‑grown health‑care workforce.  
  4. Reconnect the hospital and the community.  
- **Leadership:** The volunteer team is led by **Dr. Judy Gieske**, a retired health‑care professional, together with two other unnamed retired health‑care professionals and additional volunteers.  

### Contextual Information
- Construction of a **new modern inpatient medical facility** next to the Talbot Community Center has begun; slated to open in **2028**.  
- **Memorial Hospital** currently relies heavily on traveling doctors and nurses, highlighting the need for locally trained health‑care professionals.  
- Historically, the **McQueen‑Gibbs School** trained nurses locally, illustrating past local health‑care education capacity.
