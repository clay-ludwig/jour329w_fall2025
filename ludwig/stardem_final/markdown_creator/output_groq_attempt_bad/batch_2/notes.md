## Story 1 – Mountaire Farms $2,500 Scholarships (June 16 2024)

**Company / Program**
- **Mountaire Farms** – nation’s fourth‑largest chicken company.  
- **Scholarship amount:** $2,500 per student (32 students).  
- **Eligibility:** Children and grandchildren of Mountaire employees, growers, and grain producers.  
- **Recognition event:** Faith and Family Night at the Delmarva Shorebirds (students honored on the field and dined with Mountaire executives).  

**Key Quote**
> “We are proud of this program because it embraces everything that we care about — our employees, our growers, our grain producers and their families,” said **Phillip Plylar**, President and CEO, Mountaire Farms.  
> “And these students have all demonstrated a commitment to their education, and to their communities, which we believe should be celebrated.” – Phillip Plylar
