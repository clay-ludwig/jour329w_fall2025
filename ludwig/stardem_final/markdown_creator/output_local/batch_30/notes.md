## Caroline County Education - Beat Book Notes (December 14, 2025)

**Story 1: Caroline County schools celebrate teachers**

*   “Caroline County Public Schools honored teachers and support staff during Teacher Appreciation Week in May 2025.”
*   “The week featured free coffee and visits from district employees at each school.”
*   “Caroline County Board of Education President Michele Wayman emphasized the importance of recognizing educators' dedication and hard work.”
*   “Danielle Dennis, 2024-25 Teacher of the Year, was a math teacher at Colonel Richardson High School and said she became a teacher to make a difference in her community.”
*   “Amy Bauman, Educators Association President, reflected on the challenges faced by teachers and highlighted their role in inspiring students.”

**Story 2: Local Student Selected For General Assembly Page Program**

*   “William O’Donnell, an Easton High School senior, has been chosen to represent Talbot County in the Maryland General Assembly Student Page Program for the 2024 Legislative Session.”
*   “He previously served on the Maryland Youth Advisory Council and holds various leadership positions.”
*   “The program was established in 1970 and selects 105 student pages annually from across Maryland.”

**Story 3: TCPS gives Blueprint updates**

*   “The Talbot County Board of Education meeting shared updates on the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future plan.”
*   “The Maryland State Department of Education has approved the College and Career Readiness Standard.”
*   “Helga Einhorn presented two options for students to be considered college and career ready: earning a high school GPA of 3.0 or scoring proficient or above in Algebra I and English Language Arts.”
*   “The new standard goes into effect immediately, with an interim standard remaining in place until July 1, 2025.”

**Story 4: St. Michaels reading specialist receives Fulbright award**

*   “Kelly Coutts, a reading specialist at St. Michaels Elementary School, received a Fulbright Teacher Exchange award for the 2024-2025 cycle from the U.S. Department of State and the Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board on September 20, 2024.”
*   “Coutts will participate in an online course and research abroad, with details of her destination to be revealed later.”
*   “The award recognizes her hard work and dedication to Talbot County students, following a tradition of over 800 U.S. citizens who teach, study or conduct research abroad through the Fulbright Program each year.”

**Story 5: Delean-Botkin resigns from Talbot Board of Education**

*   “Susan Delean-Botkin stepped down from Talbot County Board of Education on August 4, 2023, due to personal reasons and her new role as Oxford Commissioner.”
*   “She was sworn into the Talbot County Board of Education seat in January 2017 and served as president until December 2022.”
*   “The governor’s office will appoint a new board member to fill Delean-Botkin’s seat until elections in November 2024.”

**Story 6: Talbot County Free Library hosts youth STEAM Camp**

*   “Talbot County Free Library hosted a two-week STEAM camp for elementary and middle school students in Easton, MD.”
*   “The program was designed by three local high school students and funded by a grant from the Council of Friends of the Talbot County Free Library.”
*   “The camp included activities such as tie-dyeing shirts, print-making tote bags, and making slime, with volunteers guiding and assisting the campers.”

**Story 7: Dorchester BOE votes to keep staffing Positive Change Academy through school year**

*   “Dorchester County Public Schools will continue to provide alternative education through its Positive Change Academy with staffing provided by PASS Educators, a substitute and permanent staffing provider from Delaware.”
*   “The program serves pre-K through second graders in the district.”
*   “A new agreement allows the current staff to finish out the school year after Transforming Lives' contract was set to expire imminently.”

**Story 8: A dream come true: Italian exchange student spending a year in Maryland**

*   “Emma Agazzani, a 17-year-old Italian exchange student from Mantua, Italy, will spend a year in Maryland as part of the American Scandinavian Student Exchange program.”
*   “She was accepted in February 2022 and is currently attending Huntingtown High School.”
*   “Agazzani has been able to experience cheerleading, lacrosse, and other extracurricular activities not available in Europe.”
*   “She plans to study science in college, potentially becoming a neurologist, after completing her secondary education in Italy.”

**Story 9: KCHS students explore STEM careers with local businesses**

*   “Kent County High School students participated in a STEM job shadowing program with eight local businesses, including Chesapeake CNC and University of Maryland Shore Medical Center.”
*   “Zane Carter has supported the program for 13 years.”
*   “The program allows employers to outline educational requirements and real-world demands to students planning their futures.”

**Story 10: Talbot, Kent see rise in student homelessness**

*   “Kent County and Talbot County in Maryland saw the largest year-to-year increase in student homelessness, with Kent County’s numbers jumping 55% to 102 students and Talbot County’s increasing by 39% to 324 students.”
*   “The Eastern Shore districts of Dorchester (3.6%), Queen Anne's (2.9%), and Caroline (2.2%) reported lower rates.”
*   “Nonprofit shelters on the Eastern Shore, such as Talbot Interfaith Shelter and His Hope Ministries, are seeing an increase in calls for assistance, with difficulties in serving older youth without guardianship or family support.”

**Story 11: Two face off for District 3 Dorchester County Board of Education position**

*   “Christine LaMonica is running for the District 3 Dorchester County Board of Education seat.”
*   “She wants to address safety concerns in schools and advocate for quality education for all students.”
*   “LaMonica believes the board needs to promote transparency and collaboration in decision-making.”
*   “Christine LaMonica stated her goals include improving communication between the school board and the community.”
*   “Christine LaMonica also wants to improve the district’s focus on student safety and academic outcomes.”

*   “Phil Whitelock is running for the District 3 Dorchester County Board of Education seat.”
*   “Rebecca Redmer and Phil Whitelock are piloting Risk Threat Crisis' Violence Prevention Initiative at North Dorchester High School to combat student violence.”
*   “The initiative aims to reduce youth violence, strengthen communication, and build collaboration among schools and communities.”

**Story 18: Lockerman Middle School celebrates new STEM Center**

*   “Lockerman Middle School in Denton, MD, celebrated its new STEM Center with a ribbon-cutting ceremony on December 23, 2024.”
*   “A $500,000 grant from the Cal Ripken, Sr. Foundation funded the center, providing students with resources such as 3D printers, coding robots, and electronic kits.”
*   “The University of Maryland Medical System partnered with the foundation to support the initiative.”
*   “Lockerman Middle School’s superintendent, Derek Simmons, and principal, Jeannine Necessary, praised the grant for bringing unique opportunities to Lockerman students.”

**Story 19: ‘Green School’ programs in Caroline County bring joy to students**

*   “Two Caroline County public schools, Denton Elementary and Greensboro Elementary, have been certified as Green Schools by the Maryland Association for Environmental and Outdoor Education.”
*   “The certification process took two years to complete and involved collaboration with community partners, including organizations such as Pickering Creek Audubon Center and Chesapeake Bay Foundation.”
*   “Both schools have implemented projects aimed at reducing waste, conserving energy, and promoting sustainability, such as composting programs and garden initiatives.”
*   “Green Schools certified since 2021, both schools aim for every school to obtain certification.”

**Story 20: Fifth-grader recognized with Hero Award for saving classmate’s life**

*   “James Phillips, a fifth-grader, received a Hero Award for potentially saving his classmate’s life in the cafeteria at Conowingo Elementary School on December 11.”
*   “Dr. Jeffrey Lawson, Cecil County Public Schools Superintendent, honored Phillips for his bravery and dedication to helping others.”
*   “The incident occurred during lunch when one student started choking, and Phillips performed the Heimlich maneuver successfully.”
