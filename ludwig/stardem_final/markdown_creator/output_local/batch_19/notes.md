## Caroline County Education Beat Book - Notes

**STORY 1: Dorchester County Board of Education discusses student devices**

*   Dorchester County Board of Education revisiting student device usage policy.
*   Pilot program implemented last October at three DCPS middle schools.
*   Board voted to eliminate pouches used by students, who circumvented them.
*   Alternative solutions being planned (locking devices in lockers or backpacks).
*   Final decision on alternative solution will be made on August 21, 2025.

**STORY 2: Maryland stories: This reading club is going to the dogs**

*   Southern Maryland-based Pets on Wheels, started by Johns Hopkins in 2013, uses therapy animals to help homebound patients feel less lonely and improve reading skills and literacy.
*   Program relies on volunteers who must meet specific pet qualifications, including the “canine good citizenship test.”
*   Coordinator Sharon Stapf leads the organization.

**STORY 3: TCPS looks into creation of clay target shooting club**

*   Talbot County Board of Education heard from five public members supporting the creation of a high school clay target shooting club.
*   TCPS Chief Financial Officer Sarah Jones reported ongoing research into feasibility.
*   Concerns about financial liability and risk management.
*   Talbot Rod & Gun Club President Joe Capozzoli requested approval in October.
*   Discussion held at February MABE meeting.
*   Annual meeting scheduled for late April to vote on the matter.
*   District expects answers by May.
*   Board President Emily Jackson stated MABE’s decision would determine the board’s ability to approve the club creation.

**STORY 4: Banned books once again increasing**

*   Banned Books Week celebrated for the 43rd year starting October 5-11, 2025.
*   PEN America reported 6,870 book bans enacted during the 2024-25 school year across 23 states and 87 public school districts.
*   Majority of challenged books are by authors of color, LGBTQ+ authors, women, and focus on racism, sexuality, gender, and history.
*   92% increase in challenged titles in 2023, largely driven by organized groups and politicians.

**STORY 16: ‘ArtScene the Podcast’ features QA Teacher of the Year Andrea Schulte**

*   Andrea Schulte, 2023 QACPS Teacher of the Year at Kent Island High School, was featured in “ArtScene the Podcast.”
*   Schulte studied art therapy in college and became a nationally renowned art educator.
*   Her student-centered approach and passion for helping students with developmental disabilities are key to her success.
*   Her National Art Honor Society students earned over $1 million in scholarship offers.
*   Her advanced art students thrived with a 100% pass rate on AP portfolios.

**STORY 17: Job opportunities, money available for graduates**

*   Maryland’s Department of Service and Civic Innovation offers job opportunities for graduating high school seniors or former graduates with a minimum salary of $15/hour and health insurance.
*   Youth can apply later in May.
*   State delegates and senators offer financial aid through the Commission on Higher Education (mhec.maryland.gov).
*   Many resources go unclaimed due to lack of awareness or motivation among applicants.
