## Caroline County Education Notes - December 14, 2025

*   Dorchester County Public Schools Superintendent Jymil Thompson visits classrooms and schools weekly to create a comfortable atmosphere of teaching and learning, offering guidance when needed.
*   Thompson aims to improve student success by focusing on instructional leadership, targeting a ranking increase from 23rd to 15th in four years.
*   Dorchester County Public Schools identified steps to achieve this goal, including coaching and professional development support for administrators.
*   The district had already served over 3,000 students on free meals before implementing Community Eligibility Provision (CEP).
*   Caroline County Public Schools are now serving free breakfasts and lunch to all students due to participation in the Community Eligibility Provision program of the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Food and Nutrition Service.
*   Beth Brewster, supervisor of food services, stated that the district had already served over 3,000 students on free meals before implementing CEP.
*   Caroline County school cafeterias will still charge for extra items like snacks.
*   The sixth annual STEM Festival was held at Chesapeake College in Wye Mills, MD, from November 9-11, 2023.
*   The event, part of the Maryland STEM Festival 2023, offered free admission and sessions on various STEM topics such as 3D printing, Python coding, and lego robotics.
*   Navonne Owen, UME/4-H educator for Caroline County, headed the event with a crew of educators from Mid-Shore colleges and institutions, attracting approximately 250 students and adults.
*   The festival aimed to promote STEM skills and interests through hands-on activities and competitions.
*   The University of Delaware agreed to pay $700K to settle claims it failed to disclose professor's ties to China.
*   Xiao-Hai Yan is a faculty member at Xiamen University in China.
*   The settlement does not constitute an admission of liability by UD, but rather a strategic decision to avoid litigation.
*   The University of Delaware is requesting $43.5 million from the state legislature for capital projects, including building Biden Hall and renovating aging laboratories.
*   The university is asking for $20 million to build Biden Hall, an interdisciplinary social sciences building named after former President Joe Biden, with a focus on technology and academic growth.
*   UD is also seeking $20 million to address deferred maintenance of its laboratories and other buildings, which have accumulated a backlog of $1.2 billion in needed repairs and upgrades.
*   The university's remaining request of $3.5 million will go towards renovating lab and classroom space in Georgetown and Lewes.
*   Dorchester County Public Schools announced teacher awards finalists for the 2024-2025 school year.
*   The finalists include Emma Brant, Jennifer Filopovic, Katelyn Robinson, Christy Starling, Christie Carels, Colleen Carney, Anthony Thomas, Kaylen Trice, Kierra Farrare, Crystal Carpenter, Jordan Lewis, Devyn Thompson, Haley Wright, Brianna Sann.
*   Winners will be announced at a reception on March 26, 2024.
*   The Chesapeake Multicultural Resource Center awarded scholarships to 14 students.
*   Morgan Lewis, who plans to pursue a master's and doctoral degree in psychology, was a scholarship recipient.
*   Open Doors Partners in Education continues to provide free and low-cost tutoring and literacy support, with over 50 attendees at its open house on February 3, 2025, in Chestertown.
*   Open Doors utilizes the Orton-Gillingham approach, a multi-sensory approach designed to help struggling readers.
*   Open Doors has 57 students enrolled and was recognized as Organization of the Year in 2023 by Kent County Chamber of Commerce.
*   American Legion Auxiliaries celebrated winners of the Americanism essay contest on May 8 in Queenstown, Maryland, recognizing students including Kaleb Brewer, Cash McQuay, and Piper Waldman as national winners.
*   Keller Scott Daniels from Preston was named to Radford University's Fall 2022 Dean's List.
*   Mackenzie Werner of Ridgely received a Bachelor of Science degree in Elementary/Middle School Dual Certification from Frostburg State University in December 2023.
*   Kyle Hofmann, a Greensboro resident, was named to Husson University's Dean's List for the Fall 2022 semester.
*   The Rotary Club of Cambridge visited the Richardson Maritime Museum and Ruark Boat Works on July 23, 2024.
*   Rotarian Dave Williams gave the guests a tour of the facilities, explaining their Educational Program, which teaches young people about woodworking and tool use.
*   A committee called the Education Specifications Committee was formed in April 2025 to discuss renovating or replacing Lockerman Middle School in Easton, Maryland, as part of Caroline County Public Schools' 2024-2026 construction project.
*   Superintendent Dr. Derek Simmons leads the committee, which includes stakeholders from schools, community members, and state officials.
*   Consultant David Lever will use the committee’s documents to design the school, which would receive $76 million in funding from the state, with the county responsible for the remaining 11%.
