## Caroline County Education Notes - December 14, 2025

*   Dorchester County Board of Education terminated its one-year contract with Transforming Lives, ending alternative education services for up to 48 elementary and middle school students.
*   Termination was effective due to the contract’s 60-day advance written notice requirement.
*   This followed the board’s termination of its three-year contract with Vision Quest in June 2024.
*   Caroline County students earned the Global Seal of Biliteracy on December 5, 2023.
*   17 Caroline County students earned the Global Seal of Biliteracy.
*   Mirta Valdes-Bradner, North Caroline High School Spanish language teacher, received the 2023 Minority Recognition Award from the Maryland State Educators Association in June 2023.
*   The Class of 2024 at Kent County High School consisted of students heading to colleges or trade schools (70%), entering the workforce (26%), enlisting in the military (4%), and others.
*   Valedictorian Ben Loller will attend Brown University to study computer science.
*   Salutatorian Madison Messick earned a full ride to the University of Maryland.
*   Chesapeake College at Queen Anne’s Tech building rebuilding project is a state-funded initiative requiring approval from three counties, including Caroline County.
*   Caroline County will contribute $2.7 million over three years to the project.
*   Chesapeake College President Clifford Coppersmith expects the state to cover 75% of costs.
*   The commissioners decided to table the discussion until the end of the budget workshop.
*   The seventh cohort of Advanced Manufacturing Program students at Caroline Career and Technology Center presented design ideas for their dartboard cabinet project on October 26, 2023.
*   Rashad Wilson’s winning design was inspired by saloon doors in the Old West.
*   The program began three years ago when a student approached Keith Hale with the idea to manufacture dartboard cabinets.
*   North Caroline High School graduate Sa'Mauria Ennals simultaneously completed an advanced transfer certificate from Chesapeake College and became a certified nursing assistant.
*   Sa'Mauria Ennals faced challenges getting enrolled in courses but eventually worked with TRIO Upward Bound program coordinators, including Gabrilla Moreland and Mark Berry, to prove her abilities.
*   Moms for Liberty (M4L) was formed in Maryland during the COVID-19 response to address concerns over mask mandates and school closures.
*   M4L members expressed concern over books in elementary school libraries with explicit content and dismal proficiency scores in math and English.
*   Talbot County Public School District recognized over 100 community partners at a breakfast event on April 26, 2024, in Easton, Maryland.
*   Notable recipients included the Talbot County Public Library, Rack Room Shoes, Second Chance Thrift Shop, Foundation of Hope, and the St. Michaels Fire Department.
*   Hundreds of backpacks stuffed with school supplies were handed to families ahead of the first day of school at Easton Middle School's indoor resource fair.
*   The event was hosted by For All Seasons and saw over 25 community organizations participate.
*   800 backpacks were prepared for distribution, which will also be delivered to local schools for those not picked up in person.
*   Carly Palmer, outreach coordinator, said the giveaway helps equip families with supplies throughout the year, while Beth Anne Dorman stated it makes students more confident on day one of school.
