## Caroline County Education Beat Book - Notes (December 14, 2025)

**Story 1: Superintendent shares schools' budget constraints with Commissioners**

*   Caroline County Public Schools officials shared a $3.25 million surplus with the Caroline County Commissioners on January 16, 2024.
*   Superintendent Dr. Derek Simmons stated that support services salaries are not addressed in the Blueprint for Maryland's Future unfunded mandate.
*   The school district faces fiscal challenges due to the Blueprint for Maryland's Future unfunded mandate and declining pandemic-based grant funding in fiscal year 2025.

**Story 4: Parties should stay out of school elections**

*   The Maryland Democratic Party will spend tens of thousands of dollars targeting school board candidates it claims support a "hateful" and "right-wing agenda."
*   The party plans to hire canvassers and provide resources to local Democratic central committees for supporting candidates with "Democratic and inclusive values."
*   Some argue that state and local political parties should not be involved in non-partisan school elections.

**Story 8: Easton High teacher named finalist for Maryland Teacher of the Year**

*   Jeremy Hillyard, a Spanish and English as a second language math teacher at Easton High School, is one of seven finalists for Maryland Teacher of the Year.
*   He was named Talbot County Public Schools' 2024-25 Teacher of the Year in April.
*   Hillyard will attend a reception in Baltimore on October 4 to compete for the top award.

**Story 6: PoliTalks transitions leadership as new era begins at Washington College**

*   Washington College's PoliTalks transitions leadership with co-presidents Charlotte Schurman and Grace Walsh taking over as new era begins.
*   The club, founded by Stephen Hook and Zach Affeldt, aims to provide a safe forum for political education and conversation.
*   Heather Mizeur delivered a lecture on "Sovereignty" at the Hynson Lounge on April 24.

**Story 2: 'This place impacts lives': Talbot County Free Library celebrates 100th birthday**

*   Talbot County Free Library celebrated its 100th birthday on October 17, 2025, with two books missing from the shelves for 50 years being returned by state senator Johnny Mautz.
*   Caroline Burnite Walker founded the library in 1925.
*   Talbot County Council Vice President Pete Lesher reflected on how the library guided his research projects and skills, stating it impacts lives.

**Story 5: Dorchester County Council president, VP thank Board of Education leadership after resignations**

*   Dorchester County Council President Lenny Pfeffer and Vice President Mike Detmer thanked Dorchester County Board of Education members Mike Diaz and Susan Morgan for their service on July 2, following their resignations.
*   Diaz resigned June 28 due to family and health concerns after undergoing a liver transplant in February.
*   Morgan resigned June 14 amid controversy over terminating the Vision Quest contract, citing dissatisfaction with the Council's direction.
