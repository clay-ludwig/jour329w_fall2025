## Caroline County Education Beat Book - Notes (December 14, 2025)

**STORY 1: TCPS superintendent addresses mailers questioning district spending**

*   “Talbot County Public Schools Superintendent Sharon Pepukayi addressed mailers questioning district spending.”
*   “There were inaccurate monetary figures in the mailers.”
*   “Acknowledging work to be done in math education, which is a priority.”
*   “The mailers, sent by Pay Attention America Inc., contained claims of low student proficiency rates and questioned the district’s spending.”
*   “A Board of Education work session in September will show recent state testing data, contradicting some claims made in the mailers.”

**STORY 3: Backpack giveaway draws big crowd in Goldsboro**

*   “CASA of Caroline and Caroline Pride partnered to host their third annual Backpack Giveaway in Goldsboro on August 30.”
*   “110 backpacks filled with school supplies were distributed to students in need.”
*   “The Town of Goldsboro donated money and provided crafts and activities for kids.”
*   “The event aimed to set up students for success by ensuring they had necessary supplies.”

**STORY 4: Karla Wieland-Cherry**

*   “Karla Wieland-Cherry, a 55-year-old resident of Trappe, is running for the District 5 seat on the Talbot County Board of Education.”
*   “She aims to improve the local school system by attracting more qualified teachers, raising education standards, and increasing the use of CTE programs.”
*   “Wieland-Cherry prioritizes rebuilding trust with teachers and addressing safety concerns in schools.”

**STORY 7: Carey Wright appointed interim state superintendent**

*   “The Maryland State Board of Education voted unanimously to appoint Carey Wright as interim state superintendent on October 6, 2023, effective October 23, 2023.”
*   “Wright will serve until June 30, 2024.”
*   “Wright previously served as state superintendent in Mississippi for nine years, improving the Quality Counts grade from F to C and literacy rates.”
*   “Wright has a Maryland educational background, including stints in Prince George’s, Howard, and Montgomery County Public Schools.”

**STORY 17: ‘Closing gaps’: TCPS data shows some literacy, math improvements**

*   “Talbot County Public Schools' test score data from the 2024-2025 school year shows improvements in math and English literacy for some students, particularly Black and Hispanic students.”
*   “The percentage of kindergarten students scoring above the 50th percentile in math increased to 68% in the 2024-2025 school year.”
*   “Superintendent Sharon Pepukayi attributes the gains to improved curriculum and teacher dedication.”
*   “Pepukayi aims to address ongoing trends by focusing on the elementary-to-middle transition and hiring reading and math coaches for middle schools.”
