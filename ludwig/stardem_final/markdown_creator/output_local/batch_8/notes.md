*   **Project PRESS:**
    *   Project PRESS is a pilot program to increase and diversify the school social work labor force, particularly in underserved communities.
    *   The program received a $2.6 million federal grant.
    *   The program has placed 22 second-year master's students in schools in Washington D.C. (Wards 7 and 8).
    *   Project PRESS focuses on culturally responsive training, trauma-informed care, and creating treatment plans for students with socio-emotional challenges.

*   **Building African American Minds Inc. (BAAM) Academic Center:**
    *   BAAM’s new academic center in Easton, Maryland is expected to open in January 2025.
    *   The project is funded by private donors, community donations, and state and federal funding.
    *   The center will house classes, tutoring, youth programs, and more.
    *   Dina Daly has been leading the organization since its founding in 2005.
    *   The new center is expected to significantly expand the nonprofit's reach and services, including adult workforce programs hosted by Chesapeake College.

*   **'Blueprint for Maryland’s Future':**
    *   Maryland's 'Blueprint for Maryland's Future' is a $30 billion plan to improve public schools.
    *   The Blueprint was passed in 2021 by the General Assembly.
    *   The Blueprint aims to boost teacher salaries, expand pre-kindergarten, adjust the curriculum, implement a "ninth-grade tracker" system, and boost funding.
    *   Governor Wes Moore has acknowledged that the plan may need adjustments due to long-term deficits projected by the state Department of Legislative Services.

*   **School Board Looks at Changing Name of School:**
    *   The Charles County Board of Education reviewed changing the name of General Smallwood Middle School due to concerns over Gen. William Smallwood's ownership of slaves.
    *   Gen. Smallwood was born in 1732 and served as a delegate and governor before joining the Revolutionary War effort.
    *   The school system plans to survey the community from May to early June and consider costs for renaming, which could range from $64,500 to $124,500.
    *   The Charles County Board of Education will gather feedback on potential name changes in June.

*   **Leonard Hall Junior Naval Academy Closure:**
    *   Leonard Hall Junior Naval Academy closed after lease termination by St. Mary’s County commissioners due to maintenance issues and financial sustainability concerns.
    *   The school was paying $12,000 in annual rent.
    *   Headmistress Sarah Green submitted plans for a grant and repairs, which were deemed insufficient.
    *   Commissioner Mike Hewitt stated that subsidizing the cost of a private school is not in the best interest of all citizens.

*   **Moms for Liberty Targeting Talbot County Public Schools:**
    *   Talbot County Public Schools are being targeted by Moms for Liberty, an extremist organization that advocates against rights and equality.
    *   The Southern Poverty Law Center has designated Moms for Liberty as an extremist group.
    *   Candidates endorsed by Moms for Liberty in Talbot County include Anne O’Conner, Kerry Gunshenan, and Karla Wieland Cherry.

*   **Charles County School Board Survey:**
     * The Charles County Board of Education plans to survey the community from May to early June and consider costs for renaming.

*   **Caroline County School Closures:**
    *   Caroline County Public Schools closed January 21, 2025; Talbot County Public Schools operate on two-hour delay that same day.
    *   Offices remain open for both school districts, while Dorchester County Public Schools are unaffected.
