**STORY 3: Chesapeake College works to become Hispanic-Serving Institution**

*   Chesapeake College aims to become a Hispanic-Serving Institution.
*   Zulema Delacruz Martinez, a 2024 graduate and founder of the Hispanic/Latino Student Union, credits TRiO programs for her success.
*   The college has seen a 64% increase in its Hispanic population over the last decade, with over 90% of attendees at FAFSA Nights applying to Chesapeake College.
*   Zulema Delacruz Martinez was awarded the John T. Harrison Award and stated that she wants to help other students from similar backgrounds succeed.

**STORY 8: Uncommon classes: How pop culture imagines Jesus, from cartoons to musicals - Images of Jesus have always been complex and contradictory**

*   Jeannie Sellick designed a course "Jesus in the Modern Imagination" to explore the relationship between religion and media.
*   Comparing ancient depictions of Jesus to modern films, comics, and music.
*   Examines gaps in the Bible's narrative about Jesus’ childhood and adolescence.
*   Using apocryphal sources like the Infancy Gospel of Thomas.
*   Students discuss how interpretations of religious figures are shaped by communities that imagine and re-imagine them.
*   A focus on the complexities of Jesus’ image.

**STORY 10: PES teacher wins McNeely Award**

*   Christi Saylor, a physical education teacher at Preston Elementary School in Denton, received the Simon A. McNeely Award from SHAPE Maryland on November 7, 2023.
*   She was recognized for her outstanding teaching and service innovations as well as being actively involved in school and community affairs.
*   Saylor must have been a member of SHAPE Maryland or a related organization with at least five years of professional experience to be eligible for the award.

**STORY 11: College built as an economic engine for Shore**

*   Chesapeake College was founded in December 1965 as a community college to address regional economic and workforce needs on Maryland’s Eastern Shore.
*   The college is part of the American Dream concept, connecting individuals to jobs and education.
*   Gov. Wes Moore visited the Wye Mills campus in April 2025 with Lt. Gov. Aruna Miller and Secretary Sanjay Rai to highlight Chesapeake’s role as a regional hub.
*   The college has since become a workforce champion, supporting students and the local economy with innovative training facilities and programs.

**STORY 13: No primary election to be held for Caroline County Board of Education seats**

*   Caroline County's Board of Education District 2 will be decided in the 2024 General Election, skipping a primary election due to fewer than three candidates filing for candidacy.
*   Stefanie Johnson is the sole candidate running under the committee "Stefanie Johnson for Caroline BOE".
*   The general election is set for November 5, 2024, allowing residents to vote in person or by mail.

**STORY 14: How will Talbot County schools regulate AI this year?**

*   Talbot County schools outlined new guidelines on artificial intelligence use for the 2025-26 school year.
*   Director of Teaching and Learning Robin Werner presented guidelines at a Board of Education meeting on August 20, 2025.
*   The guidelines include encouraging responsible AI tool use by staff, prohibiting classroom decisions, safe procedures for students, and prohibited uses of AI.
