## Caroline County Education Notes - December 14, 2025

*   **Rotary Club of Cambridge:** Lucas Thorpe, co-founder and executive director of All4Love, spoke to Rotary Club members about the organization’s work with youth in Dorchester County. All4Love was founded 10 years ago by Thorpe and Khail Johnson, based on a brotherly love approach to youth mentoring.
*   **All4Love Programs:** The organization aims to improve the lives of adolescents and young adults in the Cambridge community through high school mentoring, flag football, breakfast clubs, and after-school music programs.
*   **Calvert Schools - Hoods & Cellphones:** Calvert County Public Schools will ban students from wearing hoods in school buildings starting August. Majority support for the ban was cited. The policy change also affects cellphone usage, requiring devices to be powered off and kept secure during school hours.
*   **DCPS Staff Reductions:** Dorchester County Public Schools is considering staff reductions due to a $5.59 million deficit for fiscal year 2026. Superintendent Jymil Thompson aims to reduce staff by 12-24 positions to save between $900,000 and $1.8 million. The district is reviewing attrition rate trends and will negotiate with staff representatives to potentially save an additional $3.5 million.
*   **Lockerman Middle School Site Selection:** The Lockerman Middle School Committee previewed two potential new locations and a third option of staying at the current location on May 14, 2025. The first location is behind North Caroline High School, and the second is on Double Hills Road in Denton. Community members expressed concerns and suggestions for both sites, including incorporating historic value into the new school and addressing safety issues. A public meeting will be held on June 2 to gather more feedback before a decision is made by the Board of Education on July 1.
*   **Chesapeake College Brand:** Chesapeake College launched a new brand this academic year, highlighting its role in preparing students for "what's next." The college empowers individuals with skills to succeed, strengthening local economic growth and creating pathways to prosperity. Chesapeake College collaborates with partners, including K-12 education, four-year institutions, and businesses, to create curricula that resonate with real-world demands. The college boasts a 100% pass rate in its nursing program and has expanded trades offerings, including an advanced manufacturing program. Enrollment is up 9.5% this year.
*   **Early Head Start Cut:** Charles Huester, executive director for Maryland Rural Development Cooperation, discussed Early Head Start funding cuts with Caroline County Commissioners on September 23, 2025. The program was cut due to a decision to apply as a direct recipient instead of a subrecipient, resulting in $1 million of originally requested $3 million being lost. MRDC has explored options to provide for toddlers, including introducing the Patty Center model and a fee structure option. Huester reached out to Rep. Andy Harris (R-MD) but has not received a response.
*   **Caroline County Commissioners - Early Head Start:** Charles Huester reached out to Rep. Andy Harris (R-MD) but has not received a response regarding the Early Head Start funding cuts.
