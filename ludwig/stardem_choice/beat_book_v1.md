# Education Beat Book – East on Star‑Democrat  
## Compiled 2025‑11‑16  

### Purpose  
This “beat book” is a quick‑reference guide for any reporter new to the education beat on Maryland’s Eastern Shore. It lists the people we cite most often, explains why they matter, and gives you the basics you need to start a conversation—titles, affiliations, email patterns, story angles, and practical tips. Keep it handy, update it as you meet new contacts, and use it to build relationships that make our coverage richer and faster.

## How to Use This Beat Book  

1. **Start with the top‑tier contacts** – Sharon Pepukayi, Emily Jackson, and Dr. Derek Simmons. They’ll give you the most immediate access to curriculum and policy stories.  
2. **Add a contact note after each interaction** – date, phone call summary, and any follow‑up items. Keep the notes in a shared spreadsheet so the entire education desk can benefit.  
3. **Cross‑reference** – When a story involves both district policy and state funding, reach out to both the superintendent (e.g., Dr. Simmons) *and* the governor’s office (Wes Moore) for balanced coverage.  
4. **Update regularly** – People change roles, especially board members. Verify email patterns each semester and note any departures.
