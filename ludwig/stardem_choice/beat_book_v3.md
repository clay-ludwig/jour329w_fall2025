### How to Use This Beat Book  

1. **Start with the “Known Role” entries** (e.g., Dr. Simmons, Wes Moore, Lindsey McCormick, Susan Delean‑Botkin). These individuals are the most reliable sources for official statements.  
2. **For the “Role Not Found” entries**, first verify their current position via the district or county website before reaching out.  
3. **Leverage the key topics** to align story angles with the person’s recent activity (e.g., ask Dr. Simmons about AP curriculum decisions; ask Lindsey McCormick about upcoming college‑readiness initiatives).  
4. **Use the suggested e‑mail patterns** only as a starting point; always cross‑check with official staff directories.  
5. **Quote‑ready:** When you get a response, request a short, on‑record quote that reflects the reporter’s style—clear, concise, and grounded in data.
